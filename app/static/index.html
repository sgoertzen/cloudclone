How to get a Client ID & Secret (one-time, ~5 min)
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com, create a project, and enable the Google Drive API.
  2. OAuth consent screen: choose External, add yourself as a test user, then Publish app to stay out of "Testing" (tokens in Testing expire after 7 days).
  4. Paste the Client ID and Secret below. The container only ever requests read-only Drive access.
